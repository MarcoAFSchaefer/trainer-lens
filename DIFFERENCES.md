# Differences from the unmodded game

Trainer Lens shows information the game already has. A few panels can also act, and only when you click.

## Game data and rules

Nothing. No species, moves, items, maps, text, trainers, encounters or battle rules are added or changed. Catch chances, encounter rates, IVs and EVs are read or worked out from the game's own data and formulas.

## What the unmodded game does not show you

- Exact IVs, EVs, natures and abilities of your Pokémon and of the opponent.
- The exact catch chance of each ball in a wild battle.
- The wild Pokémon of the current map with their rates, and which you have caught.
- Visible and hidden items left on the map.
- The current map and the Town Map in a panel while you walk.

Whether that fits the way you want to play is up to you: none of it is hidden behind a setting of the game itself.

## Actions (each one is a click, through the game's own functions)

- **Tools panel:** uses field moves and key items the menu would offer right now (bicycle, Cut, Surf, Fly and so on, rods, VS Seeker, Repels, Escape Rope). **Auto Repel**, off by default, uses the next Repel from the bag when one wears off.
- **Party panel:** swaps two party members; gives, takes or switches held items from the bag.
- **PC panel:** moves Pokémon between box slots and sorts the boxes you have not locked. It never deposits or withdraws.

## Screen and input

Panels are drawn in Trainer Desk docks, or in up to five desktop windows without it. Input to the game is untouched.

## Saves

The mod adds nothing of its own to the save. The actions above change the party, the bag and the boxes exactly as doing them in the game's menus would. Settings are kept in the mod's own cache.

## Turning it off

Disable the mod and the panels are gone. Nothing is left in the save.
