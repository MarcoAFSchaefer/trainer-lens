# Trainer Lens

Information panels for FireRed and LeafGreen. They read the game; the only ones that change anything do so when you click, through the game's own functions.

- **Area Pokémon**: wild Pokémon of the current map by method, level range and chance, with the ones you caught marked. Click one for its sheet.
- **Party**, **Opponent** and **My Pokémon**: types, nature, ability, item, moves, and one radar chart of stats, IVs and EVs.
- **Moves**: power, accuracy, PP, description and effectiveness against the opponent.
- **Item radar**: visible and hidden items left on the map.
- **Tools**: one click for the bicycle, field moves, rods, VS Seeker, Repels and Escape Rope; only what can be used right now is shown. **Auto Repel** uses the weakest Repel when one wears off.
- **Trainer**, **Progress**, **Pokédex**, **Bag**, **Day-Care**, **Encounter counter** and **Notices**.
- **Area map** drawn with the game's own tiles, and the game's **Town Map**.
- **PC**: the boxes and the full sheet of any Pokémon in them. View only.
- Party members can be reordered by clicking two of them.

With **Trainer Desk** the panels dock around the game and redraw themselves for the room they have; without it, up to five panels open as desktop windows.

Panels in English, Spanish, Portuguese and Japanese. Names that come from the game stay in your game's language.

Tested on FireRed; LeafGreen shares the same engine paths but has not been played with it yet.
